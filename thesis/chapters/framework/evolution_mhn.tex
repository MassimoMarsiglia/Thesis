\section{Evolution of Hopfield Networks}

The concept of associative memory has a long history in artificial neural networks. 
A foundational contribution was made by John J. Hopfield in 1982 with the introduction of the Hopfield network, 
a recurrent neural network designed to store and retrieve information through associative memory~\cite{hopfield1982}.
Unlike feed-forward neural networks, which produce an output for a given input, Hopfield networks can recover previously stored patterns from incomplete or noisy inputs by iteratively refining their internal state.
