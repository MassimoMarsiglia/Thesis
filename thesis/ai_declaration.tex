\addchap{Declaration of AI Tool Usage}

In the course of completing this thesis, supportive AI tools (Large Language Models) were employed.
The use was restricted to the following areas:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Code Development:} AI tools were used to assist with writing, refactoring, 
          and debugging software code in the accompanying project repository. The final 
          responsibility for the functionality and correctness of the code lies with the author.
          
    \item \textbf{Source Evaluation:} AI tools were used to support the assessment of which 
          sources and topic areas should be treated in greater depth within the thesis. The 
          final selection and evaluation of sources was carried out by the author.
          
    \item \textbf{Knowledge Reframing:} AI tools were used to rephrase existing knowledge 
          and to aid in understanding complex subject-matter relationships. The substantive 
          statements and technical conclusions are the result of the author's independent work.
          
    \item \textbf{Language Refinement:} AI tools were used for fine-tuning the language and 
          diction, as well as for improving grammar. The substantive design and structure 
          of the thesis rests entirely with the author.
\end{itemize}

The AI tools served exclusively as auxiliary aids. All substantive decisions, 
particularly the technical statements, evaluations, and conclusions, were made 
independently and autonomously by the author.
